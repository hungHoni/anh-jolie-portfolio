About ⌘

Anh Jolie
Saigon, VN · Fujifilm X-T4
Available for Projects

I'm Anh Jolie — a Retoucher and Graphic Designer who loves crafting luminous beauty imagery, fashion lookbooks, and expressive visual identities. Currently based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

While I value the tactile and emotional details of every frame, my work centers on non-destructive skin retouching, editorial color grading (including campaigns starring Chipu, Model Karina, Le Thanh Hoa Resort 2026, and Paula's Choice Vietnam), and cohesive brand design systems.

✨ High-End Beauty Retouch 📸 Resort & Editorial Lookbooks 🎨 Color Grading & Look Dev ✒️ Brand & Print Identity 🌿 Social Art Direction
